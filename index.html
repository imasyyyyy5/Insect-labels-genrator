The workflow

Five steps from blank sheet to printed labels.

The generator walks through a short setup wizard, then opens a full editor where any single label can still be adjusted by hand.

01Sheet Setup

Choose A4 · 5×24 and the IARI 40×12mm label size — the layout locks to your printer's page before you enter any data.

02Collector Name

Enter it once. It's printed as the last line on every label across the whole sheet.

03Locations

List one locality per line. Labels cycle through them in order, wrapping to a second line only when a name is too long to fit — the date and collector name never get pushed out of place.

04Dates, weighted realistically

Mark each date WKD weekend or WRK rare weekday. Weekend dates carry roughly 6× the weight, matching how fieldwork actually clusters — or let Smart Add fill in dates automatically, counting back from today.

05Review & Generate

Generating opens the full sheet in the editor, where any individual label can be edited, before saving to history or downloading a print-ready PNG.

💬FAQ
What is the IARI insect label format?

IARI format is a standard 40×12mm specimen label used widely in Indian entomology collections. Each label carries up to four lines: host or collection method, locality, date, and collector name.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The generator works directly in your browser — no sign-up, no login, no email required.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Install it to your home screen as an app and it continues working offline afterward.

Can I edit one label after generating the whole sheet?

Yes. Tap any label in the editor to override its host, locality, date, or collector name — the rest of the sheet stays untouched.

Is my collection data uploaded anywhere?

No. Saved sheets are kept only in your device's local history. Nothing is sent to a server.
